Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 18, 2026 · App: Elixir Video Player (com.media.elixirplayer) · Developer: Ayoub Ziane · Audience: 13+

Summary Elixir Video Player is a local-first media player intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not require user accounts or upload your media library, watch history, or preferences to developer servers. To support the app while keeping it free, Elixir Video Player offers optional rewarded video ads powered by the Appodeal SDK (excluding Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network).

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

The developer of Elixir Video Player operates no backend servers for user data and does not directly collect, store, or access:

  • Personal contact details or account credentials (such as name, email address, or phone number).
  • Your local video files, filenames, folder structure, bookmarks, or playback history.
  • Precise GPS location, contacts, microphone, or camera input.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Appodeal)

Elixir Video Player integrates the Appodeal SDK and its mediated third-party ad networks (excluding Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network) to serve optional rewarded video advertisements when you choose to watch an ad to support the app. To deliver, measure, and prevent fraud in advertisements, Appodeal and its mediated advertising partners may automatically collect and transmit the following information:

  • Advertising ID & Device Identifiers: Your Android Advertising ID (GAID) and app set/instance identifiers used for ad delivery, frequency capping, attribution, and fraud prevention.
  • IP Address & Approximate Location: Your device's IP address and coarse geographic location (country, region, or city level) inferred from your IP address. Elixir Video Player does not request or collect precise GPS location.
  • Ad Interactions & Rewarded Video Events: Ad impressions, rewarded video views and completions, and ad taps/clicks.
  • Device & Diagnostic Info: Device model, operating system version, screen dimensions, language/locale, network connection type, and SDK performance or crash diagnostics required to load and render ads reliably.

You can review how Appodeal and its partners process this data in Appodeal's Privacy Policy.

EEA, UK, Swiss & Regulated US State Consent (Google-Accredited CMP & IAB TCF v2): Where required by applicable privacy laws (including GDPR in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, as well as applicable US state privacy laws), Elixir Video Player uses Appodeal's built-in Google-accredited Consent Management Platform (CMP) compatible with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2). The consent dialog automatically gathers and respects your consent choices before personalized ads are served. Where applicable, you can review or update your consent preferences at any time directly inside the app via Settings → Ad Privacy Settings.

Your Ad Privacy Choices: You can also reset or delete your Android Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time on your device via Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → All services → Ads).

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide media library features and seamless playback, the app stores non-personal state strictly inside its private storage sandbox on your device:

  • Playback State & History: File URIs, titles, playback positions, and last-played timestamps so you can resume videos where you left off and sort by Recently Played.
  • Library Metadata & Bookmarks: Bookmarked video URIs, played/new badge status for local videos, and a lightweight lookup cache indicating whether a local video contains embedded subtitle tracks.
  • App Preferences: Interface settings including Light/Dark theme, color palette, list or grid layout, sort order, quick-select behavior, New badge expiry duration, and visible metadata badges (resolution, file size, subtitles, and file extension).
  • Thumbnail Cache: Video preview thumbnails generated locally from your media files and saved in the app's private cache directory for fast scrolling.

None of your local media metadata, bookmarks, or watch history is shared with Appodeal, its mediated ad networks, or any third party.

4. Permissions & Device Access

Elixir Video Player requests only the Android permissions required for playback and optional ad-supported operation:

  • Media Access (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO on Android 13+ / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and earlier): Used exclusively on-device via Android MediaStore to discover video files, group them by folder, read basic file attributes (title, duration, size, resolution, MIME type), generate thumbnails, and play your videos. Your media files never leave your device.
  • User-Initiated File Actions: Deleting videos from your device uses Android's Scoped Storage confirmation prompt and occurs only when you explicitly confirm deletion. Loading external subtitle files (such as .srt) uses the Android system file picker, granting read access solely to the file you choose.
  • Network & Connectivity (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used for two purposes: (1) checking network availability and loading optional rewarded video advertisements via the Appodeal SDK and its mediated ad networks, and (2) streaming video when you manually enter a link in the Play from URL feature (supporting HTTP/HTTPS, HLS, and DASH). When you stream from a third-party URL you provide, your device connects directly to that host under that provider's own privacy policy.
  • Advertising ID & SDK Network State (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Included via the Appodeal SDK and Play Services Advertising ID dependencies to read the user-resettable Android Advertising ID (where permitted by your device settings and CMP/TCF v2 consent preferences) and basic non-identifying network connection state. The app never accesses your phone number, SIM serial number, IMEI, or call state.
  • Playback Controls: Audio volume gestures, player window brightness gestures, media session controls, and Picture-in-Picture (PiP) mode operate locally during active playback without recording or exporting any data.

5. Data Retention, Backup & Deletion

All app settings, bookmarks, playback positions, and cached thumbnails remain locally on your device under your control:

  • Clear Cache or Storage: Open your device's Settings → Apps → Elixir Player → Storage & cache to clear cached thumbnails (Clear cache) or reset all preferences, bookmarks, and playback history (Clear storage).
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling Elixir Video Player permanently removes its local app storage and cache from your device (without affecting your personal video files).
  • Android System Backup: If Android Auto Backup is enabled in your device's operating system settings, Android may include app preferences in your personal system/cloud device backup. Those backups are governed by your device or OS provider's privacy policy; the developer cannot access them.
  • Advertising Data: Data processed by Appodeal and its mediated advertising networks (such as your IP address and Advertising ID) is retained and managed in accordance with Appodeal's Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android system settings at any time.

6. Target Audience (13+) & Children's Privacy

Elixir Video Player is intended solely for users aged 13 years and older (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction) and is not directed to children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not install or use this application.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used the app, please contact us so we can assist.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If future updates to Elixir Video Player alter how data, advertising, or permissions are handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated on this page with a revised date.
