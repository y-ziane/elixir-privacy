Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026 · App: Elixir Video Player (com.media.elixirplayer)

Summary Elixir Video Player does not collect, store on external servers, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app operates without user accounts, analytics, crash-reporting trackers, or advertising SDKs. All playback history and preferences stay locally on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Elixir Video Player is designed as a local-first media player. We do not collect or process:

  • Personal identifiers (such as name, email address, phone number, or account credentials).
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or fingerprinting data.
  • Location information.
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, or remote crash logs.

The application contains no third-party advertising networks, analytics services, or tracking SDKs.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide media library features and seamless playback, the app stores non-personal state strictly inside its private storage sandbox on your device:

  • Playback State & History: File URIs, titles, playback positions, and last-played timestamps so you can resume videos where you left off and sort by Recently Played.
  • Library Metadata & Bookmarks: Bookmarked video URIs, played/new badge status for local videos, and a lightweight lookup cache indicating whether a local video contains embedded subtitle tracks.
  • App Preferences: Interface settings including Light/Dark theme, color palette, list or grid layout, sort order, quick-select behavior, New badge expiry duration, and visible metadata badges (resolution, file size, subtitles, and file extension).
  • Thumbnail Cache: Video preview thumbnails generated locally from your media files and saved in the app's private cache directory for fast scrolling.

None of this local data is transmitted to the developer or any third party.

3. Permissions & Device Access

Elixir Video Player requests only the Android permissions required for core playback functionality:

  • Media Access (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO on Android 13+ / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and earlier): Used exclusively on-device via Android MediaStore to discover video files, group them by folder, read basic file attributes (title, duration, size, resolution, MIME type), generate thumbnails, and play your videos. Your media files never leave your device.
  • User-Initiated File Actions: Deleting videos from your device uses Android's Scoped Storage confirmation prompt and occurs only when you explicitly confirm deletion. Loading external subtitle files (such as .srt) uses the Android system file picker, granting read access solely to the file you choose.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Used strictly when you manually enter a network stream link in the Play from URL feature (supporting direct HTTP/HTTPS media, HLS, and DASH streams). Elixir Video Player makes no background network connections and operates no backend servers. When you stream from a third-party URL, your device connects directly to that host, which receives standard network request data (such as your IP address) under that provider's own privacy policy.
  • Playback Controls: Audio volume gestures, player window brightness gestures, media session controls, and Picture-in-Picture (PiP) mode operate locally during active playback without recording or exporting any data.

4. Data Retention, Backup & Deletion

All app settings, bookmarks, playback positions, and cached thumbnails remain on your device for as long as the app is installed. You have full control over this data at any time:

  • Clear Cache or Storage: Open your device's Settings → Apps → Elixir Player → Storage & cache to clear cached thumbnails (Clear cache) or reset all preferences, bookmarks, and playback history (Clear storage).
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling Elixir Video Player permanently removes its local app storage and cache from your device (without affecting your personal video files).
  • Android System Backup: If Android Auto Backup is enabled in your device's operating system settings, Android may include app preferences in your personal system/cloud device backup. Those backups are governed by your device or OS provider's privacy policy; the developer cannot access them.

5. Children's Privacy

Elixir Video Player does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If future updates to Elixir Video Player alter how data or permissions are handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated on this page with a revised effective date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Elixir Video Player, please open an issue or reach out via our GitHub repository.
