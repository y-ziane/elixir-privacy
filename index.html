Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 8, 2026 · App: Elixir Video Player (com.media.elixirplayer) · Audience: 13+

Summary Elixir Video Player is a local-first media player intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not require user accounts or upload your media library, watch history, or preferences to developer servers. To keep the app free, Elixir Video Player displays banner ads via Google AdMob, configured with Teen (T) content ratings and Teen age-restricted ad treatment.

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

The developer of Elixir Video Player operates no backend servers for user data and does not directly collect, store, or access:

  • Personal contact details or account credentials (such as name, email address, or phone number).
  • Your local video files, filenames, folder structure, bookmarks, or playback history.
  • Precise GPS location, contacts, microphone, or camera input.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Elixir Video Player uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google LLC, to serve banner advertisements within the library interface. To deliver, measure, and secure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and transmit the following data to Google:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (subject to your device privacy settings and Teen age-restricted treatment) and app set/instance identifiers for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse geographic location inferred from your IP address.
  • Ad Interactions: Ad impressions, views, and taps/clicks.
  • Diagnostics & Device Info: SDK crash logs, performance diagnostics, and basic device attributes (such as screen size, device model, OS version, and network connection type) required to render ads reliably.

Teen (13+) Ad Safeguards: Ad requests in Elixir Video Player are configured with a maximum ad content rating of Teen (MAX_AD_CONTENT_RATING_T) and Teen Age-Restricted Treatment (AgeRestrictedTreatment.TEEN), restricting mature ad categories and limiting ad personalization in line with Google's teen protection policies.

Your Ad Privacy Choices: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time on your device via Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → All services → Ads). Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide media library features and seamless playback, the app stores non-personal state strictly inside its private storage sandbox on your device:

  • Playback State & History: File URIs, titles, playback positions, and last-played timestamps so you can resume videos where you left off and sort by Recently Played.
  • Library Metadata & Bookmarks: Bookmarked video URIs, played/new badge status for local videos, and a lightweight lookup cache indicating whether a local video contains embedded subtitle tracks.
  • App Preferences: Interface settings including Light/Dark theme, color palette, list or grid layout, sort order, quick-select behavior, New badge expiry duration, and visible metadata badges (resolution, file size, subtitles, and file extension).
  • Thumbnail Cache: Video preview thumbnails generated locally from your media files and saved in the app's private cache directory for fast scrolling.

None of your local media metadata, bookmarks, or watch history is shared with Google AdMob or any third party.

4. Permissions & Device Access

Elixir Video Player requests only the Android permissions required for playback and ad-supported operation:

  • Media Access (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO on Android 13+ / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and earlier): Used exclusively on-device via Android MediaStore to discover video files, group them by folder, read basic file attributes (title, duration, size, resolution, MIME type), generate thumbnails, and play your videos. Your media files never leave your device.
  • User-Initiated File Actions: Deleting videos from your device uses Android's Scoped Storage confirmation prompt and occurs only when you explicitly confirm deletion. Loading external subtitle files (such as .srt) uses the Android system file picker, granting read access solely to the file you choose.
  • Network & Connectivity (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used for two purposes: (1) checking network availability and loading banner advertisements via Google AdMob, and (2) streaming video when you manually enter a link in the Play from URL feature (supporting HTTP/HTTPS, HLS, and DASH). When you stream from a third-party URL you provide, your device connects directly to that host under that provider's own privacy policy.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Declared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK on Android 13+ to read the user-resettable Advertising ID where permitted by your device settings and Teen age-restricted ad configuration.
  • Playback Controls: Audio volume gestures, player window brightness gestures, media session controls, and Picture-in-Picture (PiP) mode operate locally during active playback without recording or exporting any data.

5. Data Retention, Backup & Deletion

All app settings, bookmarks, playback positions, and cached thumbnails remain locally on your device under your control:

  • Clear Cache or Storage: Open your device's Settings → Apps → Elixir Player → Storage & cache to clear cached thumbnails (Clear cache) or reset all preferences, bookmarks, and playback history (Clear storage).
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling Elixir Video Player permanently removes its local app storage and cache from your device (without affecting your personal video files).
  • Android System Backup: If Android Auto Backup is enabled in your device's operating system settings, Android may include app preferences in your personal system/cloud device backup. Those backups are governed by your device or OS provider's privacy policy; the developer cannot access them.
  • Advertising Data: Data processed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is retained and managed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android system settings at any time.

6. Target Audience (13+) & Children's Privacy

Elixir Video Player is intended solely for users aged 13 years and older (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction) and is not directed to children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not install or use this application.

To protect teen users, ad requests are restricted to a maximum content rating of Teen (T) with Teen age-restricted treatment enabled. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used the app, please contact us so we can assist.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If future updates to Elixir Video Player alter how data, advertising, or permissions are handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated on this page with a revised date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Elixir Video Player, please open an issue or reach out via our GitHub repository.
